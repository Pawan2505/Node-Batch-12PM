## Cookie (Client-side)

* Store hoti hai **browser** ke andar.
* Visible in **browser dev tools** → easily accessible & editable.
* **Insecure** – user modify kar sakta hai.
* Size limit: **500–700KB**.
* Used for: light data like theme, language.

---

## Session (Server-side)

* Store hoti hai **server** ke upar.
* Client ko bas ek **session ID** milta hai.
* **Secure** – data encrypted & hidden from user.
* Size limit: **5–7MB**.
* Used for: sensitive info like login, cart data.
